## Scientific lineage

RAC's pattern vocabulary descends from published adversarial-clothing
research and from the noRecognition pattern library by Bill Swearingen
(hevnsnt). Pattern families implemented in `ruthless_pipeline/patterns/`
— op-art chevrons, interference lines, checkerboard, fractal and Perlin
noise, high-frequency noise, simple shapes, gradients — are independent
RAC implementations informed by that published design vocabulary.

No noRecognition code, images, datasets, or outputs are distributed here;
the NRSAL v7.0 license boundary is respected and recorded. Full attribution,
the license boundary, and the detailed research review live in
[ACKNOWLEDGMENTS.md](ACKNOWLEDGMENTS.md). The academic corpus grounding —
Sharif 2016 through Zhang 2025 — is tracked per-entry with verification
status in `ctm_registry/literature/`.
