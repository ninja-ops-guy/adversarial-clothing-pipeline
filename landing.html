THE RESEARCH QUESTION

The question is not whether a pattern looks adversarial. The question is whether a candidate survives frozen rules, deformation, held-out evaluation, production constraints, matched physical controls, and reproducible evidence handling.

SYSTEM

More than a pattern generator.
