# Responsible Use

This repository is designed for privacy-preserving apparel research and evaluation in environments the operator owns or is explicitly authorized to test.

## Allowed project scope

- Open-model benchmarking in a controlled lab.
- Owned-camera and consented participant experiments.
- Textile robustness, printability, deformation, and fashion-aesthetic research.
- Reproducible comparison of baseline and candidate patterns.

## Out of scope for this repository

- Vendor-specific bypass integrations against systems without authorization.
- Claims that a garment is "invisible" to arbitrary surveillance systems.
- Publishing unverified transfer rates as product efficacy.
- Embedding secrets, credentials, proprietary camera endpoints, or private datasets in the repository.

All reported efficacy should identify the exact model/version, test protocol, camera conditions, sample size, and uncertainty interval.
